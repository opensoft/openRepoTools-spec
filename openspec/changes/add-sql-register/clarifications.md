### The database's home, the recovery, and Raven

**Raised by:** product-advocate (P14)
**Proposal lines:** 1529–1544 (Q22); 808–815 (act 3); 475–492 (rollback)
[1146–1152].
**Concern:** For Q22: host the Postgres on Eagle, where its outage coincides
with the lanes' own, so Amendment 22's availability argument holds; make the

### The REGISTER-WRITER credential's reach

**Raised by:** product-advocate (P20)
**Proposal lines:** 587–600 (the roles); 990–1034 (Q2) [407–416].
**Concern:** With direct writes, the REGISTER-WRITER secret, in a 0600 file, is
readable by every lane process on its host. Rule 9(e) forbids a hand-run SQL
write, but nothing technical prevents one. The exposure is the same as today's
git push rights.
**Design impact:** `design.md` and the manual's roles part say so plainly and
claim no protection the design lacks. The secret stays out of every
environment a session inherits (Amendment 14(c); the test at proposal
699–716).

### The guard's 50 ms and the client's form

**Raised by:** systems-architect (concern 4)
**Proposal lines:** 642–663 (the guard as the service's client); 699–716 and
758–763 (test 10); 990–1034 (Q2(g)); 1362–1374 (Q10); 1631–1644 (Q29)
[515–516, 749–755, 1013–1015].
**Concern:** Test 10's "under 50 ms" depends on the client's form. Q2(g)'s
favoured form, a block in `lanes-edit.sh guard`, still pays bash's parse of
the helper before its arm (`code/lanes-edit.sh:16856`), the tmux read
(`:10433`) and `transcript_holders`' scan of the session records (`:9664`,
about 0.4 s). The service removes the register read, which lane 4 corrects to
about 2 s wall (`:9437`), the largest part of today's cost; the client's share
alone misses the target. A client placed before the parse would grow a second
copy of Amendment 18(h)'s liveness logic, which would drift from
`transcript_holders`.
**Design impact:** Before Q2(g) is settled, `design.md` measures today's guard
on the fixture in four parts: reaching `:16856`, tmux, `transcript_holders` and
the register read, and says which parts the service removes. It then states
test 10's 50 ms against the chosen form: either a client that runs before
`lanes-edit.sh` parses its body, with the window and liveness facts gathered by
one implementation shared with `transcript_holders`; or the target restated as
the service's answer time, with the whole hook's time recorded beside it. The
same measurement gives P5's baseline and Q29's budget its figure.

### `LANES_NO_FETCH=1` inherited by a renderer

**Raised by:** adversary-engineer (ADV-C8)
**Proposal lines:** 283–327 (where the renderer commits, and what it leaves on
a lane's HEAD); 1231–1268 (Q7) [916].
**Concern:** 61 verb calls in the caller scripts pass `LANES_NO_FETCH=1`, and
under Amendment 23's S3 that is a failed fetch for a writer. A renderer forked
with it inherited refuses every render a lifecycle act starts.
**Design impact:** `design.md` says what `LANES_NO_FETCH=1` means on
`mode=primary` (Q7), and that the renderer does not inherit it from the writer
that forks it, or does not apply it to its own fetch. A test forks a render
from a verb run with `LANES_NO_FETCH=1` and sees it render. This holds under
either reading of Q27, since the fetch is the one S3 step both keep.
