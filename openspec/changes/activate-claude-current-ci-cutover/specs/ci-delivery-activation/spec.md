## Purpose

Make the owner-approved transition from prepared delivery to a narrowly scoped,
live-qualified workflow observable without weakening coverage or inferring
authority over running lanes, external access, releases or assembly adoption.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The service migration preserves every unrelated protection
The operator SHALL keep activation false while replacing only the four legacy
required contexts in repository ruleset 25966308 with delivery bound to GitHub
Actions integration 15368. Enforcement, branch conditions, strictness, bypass
actors and all unrelated controls SHALL remain unchanged and be read back.

#### Scenario: Migration readback differs from the approved context change
- **WHEN** target or effective protection has an unexpected change or uncertainty
- **THEN** enablement SHALL stop without a bypass or inferred permission change

### Requirement: Enablement is reviewed and retains full fallback
The policy SHALL be enabled only through an exact-head reviewed config PR with
successful ordinary and reusable full CI. Missing, mixed, stale, unsupported or
uncertain evidence SHALL retain full CI. All original full coverage and explicit
disabled-mode behavior SHALL remain exercised. Main CI SHALL qualify before
the authorized live canary, with no release eligibility inferred.

#### Scenario: The flag is true but evidence does not qualify
- **WHEN** effective protection, source intent, scope or authenticated proof fails
- **THEN** the flag alone SHALL NOT omit full work or grant admission or merge authority

### Requirement: Efficient sources require the manual held-heavy queue
Claude and Codex SHALL use the named manual queue for any authorized fast-only
source, never direct-merge it on delivery success. This SHALL be disclosed as
operator policy, not GitHub-enforced queue-only exclusivity. Existing full-only
queues, dependencies and other files SHALL retain their original full path.

#### Scenario: An independently reviewed scoped source is fast verified
- **WHEN** its current head/base and explicit hosted/local review qualify
- **THEN** complete heavy qualification in the named queue SHALL still precede merge

### Requirement: Live acceptance requires complete two-member evidence
Acceptance SHALL require two actual independent main-based members, successful
fast-only source/candidate PR CI, exactly one complete heavy integration, no
duplicate full main integration and matching tested/landed prefix and final
trees. Local failure/recovery fixtures SHALL be distinguished from live evidence.
Singleton, partial, failed, reset, duplicate or uncertain results SHALL NOT count.

#### Scenario: All required live identities, jobs and trees agree
- **WHEN** both reviewed members land after the one successful heavy candidate
- **THEN** the narrow canary SHALL be recorded with exact heads, member order,
  run/attempt/artifact identities, prefix trees and final landed-tree comparison

#### Scenario: Qualification needs excluded authority
- **WHEN** progress requires a bypass, app/paid access, release, assembly-pin,
  installed-tool or running-lane change
- **THEN** the operator SHALL stop and ask rather than expand this scoped approval
